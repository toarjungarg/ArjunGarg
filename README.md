# Arjun Garg

First-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo.

Most of what I've built so far has been small vehicles and robots, and most of what I've learned came from working out why they stopped working.

### What I'm working on

I'm on a Waterloo student design team, on both the electrical and dynamics subteams. I'm learning Altium for PCB design and MATLAB for analysis, and getting more comfortable in SolidWorks and AutoCAD.

### Projects

**Three-wheeled city vehicle prototype** (SHAD 2025, Memorial University)

Our team set out to make a small vehicle efficient at downtown speeds. I calculated the RPM band the motor needed to stay in, then built a mini-prototype with CAD-modelled, 3D-printed parts. Partway through, the gearbox started failing under vibration. The housing was flexing and pulling the gears out of alignment, so I redesigned it. We won Best Prototype.

At SHAD I also led the ROV design for an underwater competition, where our team had the best time.

**FIRST Robotics, Team 1325 Inverse Paradox** (2024–25)

I worked on the drive shaft and helped track down intermittent failures across the robot. One recurring in-match shutdown looked mechanical, but it came down to a mismatch between what the controller was commanding and what the electrical system could actually deliver. The team reached the district finals at the Ontario Provincial Championship and won the Quality Award.

### Outside engineering

Now it has donation boxes across the GTA and does international distributions every year, with 2,000+ candy bags donated so far. I still run it part-time from Waterloo.
